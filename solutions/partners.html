Compass is built on "orchestrate, don't replace" — it makes the people and processes inside an organization more effective. For services businesses that advice on workforce or AI transformation, that posture is a natural fit: Compass is the intelligence layer, you are the engagement layer.

Partner workspace · Multi-client

One partner workspace. Every client org.

Partners get a single workspace to manage all client deployments. Each client's Compass org is fully isolated — separate data, separate roles, separate governance. You see the deployment status and health of each engagement; clients never see each other.

The partner view shows which clients are live, which are in pilot, and where onboarding is in progress — without requiring you to log into each client org separately.

Partner motion · How to work with us

Three ways to bring Compass to clients

We keep the motion simple. Whether you want to refer, co-sell, or embed Compass as part of a managed service, the program starts from the same place: a partner call to scope the fit.

Referral Refer
You identify the client fit and make the introduction. We close and onboard. You earn a referral credit on the contract.
  • No deployment work required
  • Access to partner deal desk
  • Referral credit on closed deal
  • Client stays in your account
Co-sell Co-sell
You lead the client conversation alongside Klaut. Shared playbooks, joint demos, and co-branded materials for the engagement.
  • Shared sales playbooks
  • Joint demo access
  • Co-branded materials
  • Partner discount on seats
  • Ongoing engagement support
Managed deploy Premier
You deploy and manage Compass for the client as part of a broader AI transformation or workforce consulting engagement.
  • Multi-client partner workspace
  • Dedicated onboarding support
  • Client health dashboard
  • Shared playbooks + training
  • Preferred pricing on client seats
  • Partner co-marketing
Partner fit · Why it works

Compass orchestrates. You lead.

Compass is built on the principle that AI should augment the people and processes already inside an organization — not replace the consultant's role in diagnosing and improving them. For agencies and consultancies, this is a natural fit.

You bring the strategy, the client relationship, and the change management. Compass brings the work-pattern data, the KPI visibility, and the performance insight that make your recommendations land with evidence instead of anecdote.

Evidence-based recommendations Org-scoped data isolation Shared playbooks
Tools integration · Client deployment

Connect to what clients already use

Tools integration means Compass connects into your client's existing stack — the project management tools, communication platforms, and productivity apps they already rely on. You do not have to convince them to switch tools. Compass reads the signals from the tools they have.

For managed deploy partners, connector configuration is org-scoped: one setup per client, managed from your partner workspace.

Getting started

A partner call is where it starts

Partner engagements are not self-serve. We scope the fit, agree on the motion (refer, co-sell, or managed deploy), and set up your partner workspace together.
