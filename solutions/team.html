Solutions · Team

Visibility across your team. Privacy intact.

For managers and their teams. Compass Team gives you a shared view of work patterns, error trends, and KPI pacing across every member — without exposing individual data where it does not belong. Leadership sees direction, not surveillance.

Managers see direction and team-level trends. Individual data stays with each member.

A team is not a collection of identical data points. Compass Team is built for that reality: each member gets a private view of their own growth, while the manager gets a shared view of the team's trajectory — never the other way around.

Error detection · Team patterns

Catch recurring errors before they become habits

Error detection and correction in Compass Team surfaces recurring mistake patterns across the team — not to flag individuals, but to identify where process or tooling needs improvement. If three members are making the same class of error, that is a system signal, not a people signal.

Compass routes correction suggestions in-line: the right resource at the moment the error is detected, not buried in a quarterly review.

Report cards · Per member

Every member gets their own honest view

Each person on the team has a private report card — a running account of where their skills are, what they have closed, and what to focus on next. The manager sees a team aggregate. The member sees their full detail. Neither view leaks into the other.

Leadership can add notes and pulse observations to their own view — these surface in pulse check-ins, not directly in the member's card.

Getting started

Onboard your team without a project

Compass Team is designed for working teams, not IT implementations. A manager books a call, we configure roles and member access, and the team starts seeing data in the first sprint.

01

Manager books a call

We confirm team size, configure the Manager and Employee roles, and set up the shared KPI frame that fits your sprint or OKR cycle.

02

Members join, tools connect

Each member connects their workspace via tools integration. Compass starts building individual baselines immediately — privately.

03

First sprint review with data

At your next sprint review, the team board is live. Error patterns are surfaced. KPI pacing is visible. Every member has their own report card to bring to the conversation.
