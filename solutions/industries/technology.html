/ 01   SaaS recovery

Recover AI &
SaaS sprawl.

Engineering and product teams accumulate overlapping AI subscriptions (multiple teams paying for the same models), idle dev-tool seats, and redundant integrations. Compass surfaces idle and overlapping spend with usage-signal evidence.

The output is a recoverable spend estimate your ops or finance team can act on. Compass does not cancel subscriptions; it gives you the evidence to make a confident call.

/ 02   Orchestration

Orchestrate,
don’t replace.

Compass does not ask you to abandon the AI and dev tools your teams already depend on. It routes tasks and signals across the tools you have — Zapier, Make, OpenAI, Claude, Gemini — under your own accounts, at your own contracts.

Bring your own API keys. Zero LLM markup. Compass is an orchestration layer, not an AI reseller. The model spend stays exactly where it is in your budget.

/ What Compass is not

Honest about
what it doesn’t do.

Not a workflow engine
Compass routes to Zapier, Make, and n8n — it does not rebuild them or replace them. Your automation logic stays in the tool it already lives in.
Not an agent framework
Compass is an orchestration and workforce intelligence layer. It is not a general-purpose agentic framework for building autonomous systems.
Not an AI reseller — BYOA, no markup
Compass uses your own OpenAI, Claude, and Gemini API keys. There is no LLM resell layer and no additional markup on model inference costs.
Aggregate and source-attributed
All workforce signals are aggregate. Every inference shows which source signals drove it. No black-box scores.

Bring your own AI accounts — $0 LLM markup. Compass orchestrates the tools you already have; it doesn’t replace them. Aggregate, source-attributed, decision-support. You stay the decider.
