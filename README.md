## Phase 01 — Consult

Executive alignment before execution.

We align executive priorities, operational realities, and AI transformation objectives into a unified strategic direction before a single system is deployed.

---

## Phase 02 — Diagnose

Operational intelligence assessment.

We conduct a comprehensive analysis of workflows, infrastructure, data readiness, and organizational constraints to identify the highest-impact AI opportunities.

---

## Phase 03 — Design

Architecting scalable AI systems.
