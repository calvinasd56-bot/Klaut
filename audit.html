/ 01   Why it matters

When the agent fails,
you own it — not the vendor.

Accountability for AI runs downhill to whoever deployed it. The vendor’s terms don’t protect you when an agent quotes the wrong price, leaks a record, or makes a decision a regulator wants to see the paperwork for.

An audit is how you find the failure modes, the unsafe paths, and the compliance gaps while they’re still cheap to fix.

And how you prove to a board or a regulator that you looked.

We work against: NIST AI RMF ISO/IEC 42001 EU AI Act UU PDP · OJK
/ 02   What’s covered

Two pieces.
One name on the report.

Code Audit

Logic, guardrails, failure modes

Review of the agent’s logic, guardrails, and failure modes — the paths where it can do something you didn’t intend.

  • Static review of agent code & tool wrappers
  • Adversarial behavior tests against your real workflows
  • Prompt-injection, jailbreak, and data-leak paths
  • Findings ranked by severity, each with a fix
2–4 weeks From $60k
Certification

A documented assessment

A documented assessment you can show to stakeholders, with the findings and the remediation tracked — renewed when the agent changes materially.

  • Signed report, EN and Bahasa Indonesia
  • Mapped to NIST AI RMF and ISO 42001
  • Remediation tracker, valid for one year
  • Re-certification when the agent changes materially
1 year validity From $25k
