/ Honesty

What Tools automation is not.

Compass is a thin orchestration and visibility layer. We’re deliberate about what we’re not trying to be.

Not a workflow engine

Klaut routes to Zapier, Make and n8n; it doesn’t rebuild them. Your workflows, triggers and connectors live in the tools you already own.

Not an agent framework

The doing happens in tools you control. Compass decides routing and order — it doesn’t run an autonomous agent on your behalf or act without a clear trigger.

Not a reseller of AI

You bring your own model accounts; there is no markup. Your API spend goes directly to the model provider under your contracts.

Not a data sink

Source attribution is always shown; nothing is hidden or relabelled. Every task and event retains its origin badge so your team knows exactly where it came from.

Compass orchestrates — it doesn’t replace your tools or run a black-box agent. You bring your own accounts; Klaut adds routing, priority and correction. Source attribution is always shown.
