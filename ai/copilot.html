/ Compass · AI Feature

A copilot that grows
when you do.

Each person gets one AI companion inside Compass. It earns $POIN from real work-loops — a lesson finished, an assessment passed, a skill gap genuinely closed. It’s yours alone; no manager ever sees it.

/ How $POIN are earned

It earns $POIN from real work,
not vanity metrics.

Every point traces back to a closed loop — a lesson completed, a skill gap verified shut. Logging in earns nothing. The companion grows in proportion to how much you genuinely learn.

compass · $poin earn · schedule
Lesson completed +15 $POIN
Assessment passed +40 $POIN
Skill gap closed (verified loop) +120 $POIN
Module finished +60 $POIN
Daily learning streak +5 $POIN

Points scale with the size of the work-loop closed — and only when the loop is genuinely verified. No points for logging in.

VERIFIED ONLY

The loop has to close.

Starting a lesson doesn’t earn $POIN. Clicking through doesn’t earn $POIN. The system waits for the full loop: lesson → quiz or review → pass — then awards. Partial work stays at zero.

PRIVATE LEDGER

Only you see your balance.

$POIN balances, earn history, and companion state are scoped to your account. No leaderboard is visible to managers or HR. Your companion’s growth is between you and your work.

/ Honesty

What the companion is not.

Honest design means being explicit about what something doesn’t do. The companion is motivational. That’s the whole job.

Not a performance score

The companion state, $POIN balance, and rarity of your cosmetics never feed a manager view, an HR dashboard, or any performance report. They are invisible outside your own account.

Not surveillance

The companion reflects only your own verified work-loops. It has no awareness of collaboration data, attendance, or behavioral signals. It sees the same slice of your learning record that you do.

Not pay-to-win

$POIN cannot be purchased with real money. There is no premium currency, no shortcut, no in-app purchase. Every point comes from a verified learning event — period.

Yours alone

Companion state stays private to you. There is no org-wide leaderboard, no team comparison, no manager-visible rank. Your companion grows in your corner of Compass, unseen by anyone else.
