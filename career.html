/ 01 — Team thesis

Lean by design — not understaffed.

Most early companies hire to feel like a company. We hire when a system can't absorb the work and a person genuinely can.

That keeps the team small, the ownership high, and the work unambiguous. If you want to be one of many, this isn't the place. If you want your decisions to matter, it is.

Hiring trigger
A system that can't absorb the work.
Not a roadmap, a board deck, or a quarter.
Hiring constraint
A person who can genuinely close the gap.
Not a seat to fill. A problem to own.
